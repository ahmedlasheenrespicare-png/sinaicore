Who We Are

Connecting North Sinai Salt to the World

SinaiCore coordinates supply from qualified marine salt producers in North Sinai, providing customers with reliable volumes, flexible specifications, and consistent export-ready products. We manage the full chain — product selection, batch testing, specification control, packaging, documentation, and shipping — supported by independent third-party laboratory verification when required.
