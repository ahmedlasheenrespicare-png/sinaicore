  wooden_crate_01            -> prop-crate.glb

  wooden_bucket_01           -> prop-bucket.glb
  plastic_container          -> prop-tub.glb

All processed by scripts/build-real-models.mjs: weld, dedup, meshopt
simplify (ground only — the props are already light), prune, 1024 px JPEG
re-encode, embedded as GLB.

What these carry that our authored meshes do not: baked albedo, normal and
AO/roughness maps. Rust, chipped paint and work marks are in the pixels, not
painted on — which is the whole difference between a plate that reads as a
photograph and one that reads as a render.

CC0 places these in the public domain: attribution is not required. It is
given here anyway, because which objects stand beside a product, and which
rock it stands on, is part of how the page reads.
