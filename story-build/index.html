02 · The pour

One continuous arc

Tip the sack and the tablets leave it one by one, curving as they fall. Every disc is pressed to the same measure, so a brine tank drawn by count is a brine tank drawn by weight.

  • Ø 25 mm diameter
  • 11 mm thickness
  • ≈ 15 g per tablet
  • Slow dissolving

The motion is tied to the scroll: stop, and the tablets hang; scroll back, and they return to the sack.

04 · Why it works

Slow by design

A tablet is a decision about surface area. Press it dense and it gives the brine a small, steady face — dissolving over weeks instead of hours, so saturation holds between refills.
